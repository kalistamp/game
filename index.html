How to Play 🧭

🛒 Buyer Mode

Tap items on the shelf to add them to your cart. Then select bills and coins to pay the exact total.

💵 Cashier Mode

A customer pays for items. First enter the correct change amount, then select the bills and coins to give back.

⭐ Pro Tips

Double-tap any bill to remove one. Use the fewest pieces possible for an Efficiency Bonus. Right-click also removes one piece.
