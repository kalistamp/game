How to Play 🧭

🛒 Buyer Mode

Look at your shopping total. Click on bills and coins to match the exact price. Don't pay too much or too little!

💵 Cashier Mode

A customer gives you a certain amount of money for items. Calculate their change (Payment - Total Price) and select the correct bills and coins to give back.

⭐ Pro Tip

Try to use the fewest number of bills and coins possible to earn an Efficiency Bonus!
